# Video Acquisition Protocol

The pipeline is designed for practical side-view pitching video.

## Recommended Setup

- Record in landscape orientation.
- Keep the full body visible throughout the pitching motion.
- Position the camera approximately perpendicular to the throwing direction.
- Use a camera-to-pitcher distance of approximately 10–15 ft when feasible.
- Position the camera approximately waist-to-chest height.
- Use a stable camera.
- Target a frame rate of at least 30 fps.
- Use adequate lighting and participant/background contrast.
- Minimize motion blur and body-part occlusion.

## Measurement Note

The system produces projected 2D kinematic measurements. Camera placement, perspective, image resolution, and out-of-plane motion can affect the resulting values. Consistent acquisition is therefore important for repeated or longitudinal comparisons.
