# Measurement Quality Control

Measurement quality is evaluated independently from mechanical characterization.

| Criterion | Threshold |
|---|---:|
| Minimum pose-detection rate | 0.90 |
| Minimum critical-landmark visibility | 0.50 |
| Minimum event confidence | 0.55 |
| Maximum critical-landmark raw gap | 100 ms |
| FFC-to-release review interval | 50–500 ms |

These thresholds are operational measurement-QC rules, not biological or clinical cutoffs.

A pitch may require measurement review because of landmark confidence, fallback event localization, or other acquisition-related uncertainty. Measurement review must not automatically increase a mechanical score or profile.
